Summary Findings:
1. Legacy HumAS-HMMER contains 99561 micro-gaps (1-3 bp) across these 9 chromosomes (56.9% of monomers).
2. Of these, 68211 (68.5%) are exact 2-bp gaps on the minus strand, directly attributable to the 1-bp coordinate shift in hmmertblout2bed.awk.
   Extrapolated to the full genome (488,754 records), this accounts for ~190,587 artificial gaps.
3. FastHumAS eliminates coordinate errors and applies consensus-anchored junction healing:
   - Flush contiguous junctions (gap = 0 bp) increase from 57220 (32.7%) to 161712 (93.0%).
   - 1-3 bp micro-gaps decrease by 92.1% (from 99561 down to 7911).
   - Artificial 2-bp minus-strand gaps decrease by 98.1% (from 68211 to 1329).
4. Remaining gaps (7,911 micro-gaps, plus genuine >3 bp interruptions) represent authentic biological variation, retrotransposon insertions, and degenerate array boundaries which FastHumAS deliberately preserves.
